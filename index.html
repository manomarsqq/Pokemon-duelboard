Battle Screen Builder

Build a Pokémon battle screen for your AO3 fic by clicking and typing. No code to write: copy the result into your chapter.

1 Install the Work Skin (once)

  1. On AO3, go to Dashboard → Skins → My Work Skins → Create Work Skin.
  2. Give it any title, paste the Work Skin into the CSS box, and submit.
  3. On your fic's Edit page, pick it under Select Work Skin and post.

Live preview

Click a Pokémon or its HP box to edit it, a Poké Ball to change it, or the message box to rewrite it. Point at a Pokémon to see its info panel, just like readers will.

3 Copy into your chapter

  1. In your chapter's editor on AO3, switch to HTML (not Rich Text).
  2. Paste the battle screen where you want it in the story, then preview or post.
Edit a battle you made before

Paste a battle screen's code here (for example, copied back out of your chapter's HTML) to keep editing it, such as for the next turn.

Your battle is saved in this browser as you work. Pokémon, move details and sprites come from PokéAPI, a free Pokémon database.
